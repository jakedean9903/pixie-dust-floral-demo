How It Works

Three simple steps to your wedding flowers.

  1. 01

    Tell us your date

    Share your wedding date, venue, guest count and budget in the inquiry form. It takes about two minutes and tells Tiffany exactly where to start.

  2. 02

    Design together

    Bring your Pinterest boards, swatches and dream flowers. Tiffany listens, adds her own ideas, and shapes a floral plan around your vision and your budget.

  3. 03

    We deliver & style

    On the big day your florals arrive fresh and are set in place at your venue, so all you have to do is walk in and gasp.
